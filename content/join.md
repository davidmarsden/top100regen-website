Top 100 Regen is a 100-club custom Soccer Manager game world run along the same lines as [Top 100](https://smtop100.blog/), with its own managers, seasons and a small number of Regen-specific rule differences.

There is no separate waiting-list form. New managers usually join by recommendation or word of mouth, or apply directly in Soccer Manager when a club becomes available.
