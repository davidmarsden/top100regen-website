# Top 100 Regen makeover — first pass

This is a small, surgical makeover rather than a rebuild.

## Identity
- Top 100 family blue remains the common visual DNA.
- Acid/lime green becomes the distinct Regen accent.
- New strapline: **Same DNA. New generation.**
- Homepage no longer presents the site as “Season 1”.
- Current season shown as **S4**.

## Structure
- Adds a thin Top 100 family navigation bar:
  Top 100 · Archive · Tournaments · Awards · Rules · Regen
- Keeps a separate Regen-local navigation:
  News · Managers · Archive · Rules · Subscribe · Join
- Adds a simple “Original / Regen” explainer on the homepage.
- Adds shared app gateways without turning the Regen site into an app directory.

## Content
- Removes stale “25 seasons / 10 years” hard-coding from the homepage/footer.
- Keeps Regen-specific Rules as the local differences layer over the canonical Top 100 rulebook.
- Youth Cup / Shield no longer occupy permanent main navigation; future competition operation belongs in Tournaments.

## Domain plan
- `top100regen.website` remains the canonical domain during the makeover.
- Planned future canonical home: `regen.smtop100.blog`.
- When the new domain is connected, update Micro.blog site settings/base URL and social-card metadata, then redirect the old domain.
